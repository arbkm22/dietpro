# DietPro - Quick Start Guide

## Welcome to DietPro! 🎉

Your personal calorie and nutrient tracking companion for iOS.

## Getting Started

### Opening the App
When you first launch DietPro, you'll see the **Today** view with:
- A calorie counter at 0
- Empty macronutrient cards
- A message inviting you to add your first meal

### Adding Your First Food

1. **Tap the green + button** in the Today view
   - Or navigate to the **Foods** tab

2. **Browse or Search**
   - Scroll through the food list
   - Use the search bar to find specific foods
   - Tap category buttons to filter (Fruits, Vegetables, etc.)

3. **Select a Food**
   - Tap any food item to see its details

4. **Adjust Servings**
   - Use the - and + buttons to adjust serving size
   - Watch the calories update in real-time
   - See all nutrients recalculate automatically

5. **Add to Today**
   - Tap the green "Add to Today" button
   - See a confirmation message
   - Return to Today view to see your entry

### Viewing Your Progress

**Today View:**
- See your total calories with a progress circle
- View macronutrient breakdown (Protein, Carbs, Fat)
- Review all meals logged today
- Each meal shows calories and time added

**Foods View:**
- Browse all 24 available foods
- Search by name
- Filter by category
- Tap any food for full details

**Nutrients View:**
- See detailed breakdown of all nutrients
- Progress bars show % of daily goals
- Color coding indicates status:
  - Green: You're meeting your goals
  - Orange: Moderate intake
  - Red: Too high (for sodium, sugar, cholesterol)

### Managing Your Log

**Deleting an Entry:**
1. Go to Today view
2. Swipe left on any food entry
3. Tap the red Delete button
4. The entry is removed and totals update

**Viewing Details:**
- Tap any food in the Foods tab
- See complete nutritional breakdown
- Both macronutrients and micronutrients displayed

## Understanding the Interface

### Tab Bar (Bottom)
- **Today** 📅: Your daily summary
- **Foods** 🍴: Browse food database  
- **Nutrients** 📊: Detailed nutrient tracking

### Color Coding
- **Green**: Calories, accents, positive actions
- **Blue**: Protein
- **Orange**: Carbohydrates
- **Purple**: Fat
- **Brown**: Fiber
- **Pink**: Sugar

### Daily Goals
The app uses standard daily goals:
- **Calories**: 2000
- **Protein**: 50g
- **Carbohydrates**: 275g
- **Fat**: 65g
- **Fiber**: 28g
- **Sugar**: 50g (try to stay under)
- **Sodium**: 2300mg (try to stay under)
- **Vitamins & Minerals**: Various RDAs

## Common Tasks

### How do I track a snack?
1. Tap + on Today view or go to Foods tab
2. Use category filter to select "Snacks"
3. Choose your snack and add it

### How do I see yesterday's meals?
Currently, the app shows today's meals only. Each day starts fresh, keeping your tracking simple and focused.

### How do I know if I'm eating enough protein?
1. Go to the Nutrients tab
2. Find the Protein card
3. Check the progress bar and percentage
4. Aim for 100% or adjust based on your goals

### Can I add custom foods?
The current version includes 24 common foods. Custom food entry is planned for a future update.

### How is my data saved?
Your food log is automatically saved on your device. Your data persists even if you close the app.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: The app shows 0 calories but I've added foods**
A: Foods added on previous days won't show today. Check that you're adding foods with the current date.

**Q: I deleted a food by mistake**
A: Simply add it back from the Foods tab. The serving size will reset to 1.0.

**Q: The progress circle isn't moving**
A: Make sure you've added foods to today's log. The circle shows progress toward the 2000 calorie daily goal.

**Q: How do I change my daily goals?**
A: Custom goals are planned for a future update. Current goals are based on standard dietary guidelines.
